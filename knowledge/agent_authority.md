# Agent Authority & Governance Boundaries

## Allowed Resolution Actions (Autonomous)
The SkyAssist resolution agent is authorized to execute the following actions without human supervisor intervention:
1. **Rebooking**: Rebook customer on next available flight within 24 hours at no charge for airline-caused disruptions.
2. **Meal Vouchers**: Issue ₹500 meal vouchers for delays > 0 hours according to policy.
3. **Lounge Access**: Issue lounge access passes for delays > 3 hours according to policy.
4. **Delayed-Hours Hotel**: Arrange hotel accommodation for the delayed-hours portion when delay exceeds 5 hours.
5. **Refund Initiation**: Process full refund to the original payment method for airline-caused cancellations (processed in 7 business days).
6. **Information Provision**: Retrieve and present booking details and flight status.
7. **Fare Waiver**: Waive voluntary rebooking fare difference UP TO ₹1,500.

## Prohibited Actions (Mandatory Escalation)
The agent must NEVER execute and MUST escalate to a human supervisor in the following cases:
1. Approving monetary compensation or vouchers beyond published policy limits.
2. Waiving fare differences exceeding ₹1,500 (e.g. ₹2,000 waiver request).
3. Making exceptions for non-airline caused disruptions or external passenger commitments.
4. Handling customer threats of legal action or formal regulatory complaints.
5. Processing refunds to a payment method different from the original booking payment method.
6. Granting full-night hotel stays when flight delay is strictly during daytime / delayed-hours portion.
7. Promising cabin upgrades (e.g., Business Class) without fare difference payment or explicit supervisor approval.
