# Policy 1: Cancellation Rebooking & Refund Policy

## Scope
Applies when a flight is cancelled by the airline due to operational, mechanical, or administrative reasons.

## Entitlements
1. **Free Rebooking**:
   - Customer is entitled to free rebooking on the next available flight operated by the airline within 24 hours of original scheduled departure.
   - Gold and Platinum loyalty members receive priority rebooking (first access to available seats).
   - Upgrades to higher cabin classes (e.g., Business Class) are NOT included under standard cancellation policy and require payment of fare difference or supervisor approval.

2. **Full Refund**:
   - Alternatively, the customer may choose a full refund of the ticket cost.
   - Refund must be processed within 7 business days.
   - Refund can strictly ONLY be issued to the original payment method.

## Options
The customer has the choice between free rebooking (within 24h) OR full refund.
