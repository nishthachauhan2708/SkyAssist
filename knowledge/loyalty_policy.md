# Policy 5: Loyalty Tier Disruption Rules

## Scope
Applies to Gold and Platinum loyalty tier members during flight disruptions.

## Rules
1. **Priority Rebooking**:
   - Gold and Platinum members receive priority seating access on the next available flights in case of cancellation or major delay.

2. **No Additional Monetary Compensation**:
   - Loyalty status does NOT grant additional cash, voucher, hotel, or upgrade compensation beyond standard published policies.
   - Status perks do not override fare waiver authority caps (₹1,500) or hotel duration limits (delayed hours only).
