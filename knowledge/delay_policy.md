# Policy 2: Flight Delay Compensation Policy

## Scope
Applies to delays caused by airline operations on the date of travel.

## Tiered Entitlements

1. **Delay under 3 hours**:
   - Customer is eligible for a ₹500 meal voucher.

2. **Delay more than 3 hours (3 to 5 hours)**:
   - Customer is eligible for:
     - ₹500 meal voucher
     - Airport lounge access pass

3. **Delay more than 5 hours (over 5 hours)**:
   - Customer is eligible for:
     - ₹500 meal voucher
     - Airport lounge access pass
     - Hotel accommodation covering ONLY the delayed-hours portion (NOT a full night's stay).

## Important Constraints
- Hotel accommodation for delays > 5h is strictly restricted to the delayed hours duration before the rescheduled flight time. It does not cover overnight full-night stays unless the delay spans overnight hours.
- Connecting flight impact, meeting misses, or external client commitments do not alter standard entitlement tiers.
