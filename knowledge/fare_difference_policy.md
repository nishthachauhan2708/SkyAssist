# Policy 4: Voluntary Rebooking & Fare Difference Policy

## Scope
Applies when a customer voluntarily requests to rebook onto a higher-fare flight or alternate route.

## Rules
1. **Fare Difference Payment**:
   - If a customer chooses a flight with a higher fare than their original booking, the customer is responsible for paying the fare difference.

2. **Waiver Authority Threshold**:
   - Resolution Agents have authority to waive fare differences up to **₹1,500**.
   - Any requested fare difference waiver exceeding **₹1,500** CANNOT be approved by the agent and MUST be escalated for supervisor review.
