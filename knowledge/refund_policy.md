# Policy 3: Refund Processing Policy

## Scope
Applies to refund requests resulting from airline-caused cancellations or eligible disruption scenarios.

## Rules
1. **Full Refund**:
   - For airline-caused flight cancellations, customers are entitled to a 100% full refund of the booking fare.

2. **Processing Timeline**:
   - Refunds are processed and dispatched within 7 business days.

3. **Payment Method Security**:
   - Refunds can ONLY be credited back to the original payment method used during booking.
   - Agents are strictly prohibited from processing refunds to alternative credit cards, bank accounts, cash, or third-party accounts.
